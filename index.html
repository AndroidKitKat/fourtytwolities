Key Features

Auto-send

Grok asks “Send this message?” when a link pre-fills a prompt. Put your token in the hash as #gwc=… and FourtyTwolities clicks Send.

Compact, wider transcript

Tighter spacing and a wider reading column on grok.com.

Hide Imagine

Removes the Imagine entry from Grok’s header.

Block video autoplay

Videos stay paused until you click them. Cover loops on Imagine stay still.

Remember your model

Restores Fast, Auto, Expert, Heavy, or Build on new chats.

No reload

Chat options live in the toolbar popup and apply on grok.com without refreshing the page.
